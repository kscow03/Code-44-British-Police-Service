Knowledge Check Standard

Knowledge checks are intended to confirm that students have completed the required learning and understand the key principles before progressing.

They should not become unnecessarily large examinations. Trainers should focus on the important learning points from each module and consider the quality of the student's understanding rather than simply the length of their answer.

Trainer Guidance

A wrong answer does not automatically require an entire module to be repeated. Where appropriate, trainers should clarify the learning point, provide feedback and reassess the specific area.

Repeated knowledge gaps, poor preparation or failure to apply feedback should be recorded as development points.

Storage Notice

Knowledge-check records are stored in browser local storage. They are therefore available only on the browser/device where they were created.

Do not use this system for real sensitive police information or genuine personal data.
