Lesson Plans

Trainer lesson plans should be delivered alongside the relevant student module. Students should complete modules in the order directed by the training team.

Phase One — Foundation

Module 1 Lesson Plan Welcome, induction, Academy structure and recruit expectations. Module 2 Lesson Plan Professional standards, integrity, accountability, communication and teamwork. Module 3 Lesson Plan Legal principles, APSTRA, decision-making, reassessment and accountability.

Phase Two — Equipment and Firearms Development

Module 4 Lesson Plan Equipment, PPE, firearms roles, vehicle familiarisation and accountability. Module 5 Lesson Plan Firearms safety, trainer commands, simulated handling and safety assessment. Module 6 Lesson Plan Operational readiness, crew communication, team awareness, threat reassessment and supervised firearms development.

Phase Three — Operational Deployment

Module 7 Lesson Plan ARV deployment information, crew responsibilities, arrival assessment, scene control, APSTRA, support requests and incident handover.

Module 9 Lesson Plan Armed containment, searches and high-risk warrants. Coming soon.

Training Delivery

Academy sessions are delivered when approved trainers, students and suitable resources are available within the city.

Modules do not require a fixed weekly timetable. Trainers should record completed learning, outstanding requirements and the student's current progression stage.

Restricted Information

Do not distribute lesson plans, expected answers, assessment criteria, scenario injects or personal recruit records outside authorised trainer channels.

This system uses basic browser-session access control. Do not store real sensitive information or genuine personal data on GitHub Pages.

Trainer Session

Trainer access remains active until the browser or tab session is closed, or until the trainer selects Logout below.
