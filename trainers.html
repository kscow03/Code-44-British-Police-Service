Lesson Plans & Assessment Packs

Trainer resources correspond directly with the 13 student Academy modules. Trainers should use the relevant lesson plan to support learning, practical exercises, knowledge checks and student development.

Not every module requires a full classroom input. Trainers should use professional judgement to determine whether the student requires discussion, demonstration, practical training or scenario-based assessment.

Phase One — Foundation

Module 1 — Welcome to ARU Induction, Academy structure, responsibilities, expectations and initial knowledge check. Module 2 — Professional Standards Integrity, accountability, professionalism, communication, teamwork and student conduct. Module 3 — Legal & Decision-Making Legal awareness, APSTRA, proportionality, continuous reassessment and accountable decision-making.

Phase Two — Equipment & Firearms Development

Module 4 — Equipment, Vehicles & PPE Equipment, PPE, firearms roles, ARV familiarisation, specialist resources and equipment accountability. Module 5 — Firearms Safety & Handling Firearms safety principles, trainer commands, simulated handling and firearms accountability. Module 6 — Firearms Fundamentals & Operational Readiness Operational readiness, crew communication, team awareness, threat assessment and supervised firearms development.

Phase Three — Operational Deployment

Module 7 — ARV Deployment & Armed Incident Response ARV deployment, crew responsibilities, arrival assessment, scene control, command updates and incident handover. Module 8 — Armed Vehicle Tactics Armed vehicle incidents, TPAC awareness, hard stops, smash-and-grab operations, Roads Policing integration and tactical reassessment. Module 9 — Armed Containment, Searches & High-Risk Warrants Armed containment, coordinated searches, high-risk warrants, scene management, specialist resources and operational handover. Module 10 — Building Operations & CQB Building operations, CQB principles, communication, team coordination, tactical movement and specialist resources.

Phase Four — Complex Operations & Qualification

Module 11 — Complex Armed Incidents & Integrated Operations Complex incidents, multi-unit operations, command awareness, casualties, NPAS, K9 and specialist capability integration. Module 12 — Consolidation & Scenario Development Academy-wide consolidation, student development, scenario exercises, remedial training and final preparation. Module 13 — Final Assessment & Qualification Final assessment framework, scenario bank, assessment injects, competency evaluation and qualification decision.

Final Assessment & Qualification

Module 13 represents the final Academy assessment. Trainers should only progress a student to final assessment once the required preceding learning and development has been satisfactorily completed.

Module 13 Assessment Pack Access the complete trainer scenario bank, assessment guidance, scenario variations and final assessment structure. Final Assessment Record Record competency performance, scenarios used, development points, final qualification decision and generate the completed training record.

Training Management System

The Recruit Tracker, Training Records and Knowledge Check Records are designed to work together.

The Recruit Tracker provides an overview of where each student currently sits within the Academy. Training Records document completed learning and practical elements, while Knowledge Check Records separately record the student's understanding of module content.

The Recruit Tracker and Trainer Dashboard use the shared Academy Supabase database so trainers can see the same recruit information.

Training Delivery

Academy sessions are delivered when approved trainers, students and appropriate resources are available within the city.

There is no requirement for modules to follow a fixed weekly timetable. Trainers should instead ensure that learning is completed in the correct order and that students have demonstrated the required understanding before progressing.

Self-directed modules should be supported by knowledge checks where appropriate. Practical modules should focus on applying Academy learning through demonstrations, exercises and changing scenarios.

Trainer Standard

The purpose of the Academy is not simply to move students through 13 modules. Trainers are responsible for ensuring that students can understand, explain and apply the principles contained within the course.

Where a student demonstrates a weakness, trainers should provide development and reassessment rather than progressing them solely because the module has been attended.

Final qualification should reflect the student's overall standard, judgement, professionalism, teamwork and operational performance.

Restricted Information

Do not distribute trainer lesson plans, expected answers, assessment criteria, scenario injects, scenario-bank information or student records outside authorised ARU trainer channels.

Students should only receive material specifically designated as student learning or authorised for release by the training team.

This system uses basic browser-session access control and Supabase database access. It should not be treated as secure storage for real sensitive information, passwords or genuine personal data.
