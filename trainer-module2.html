Trainer Module 2 — Professional Standards

Instructor Lesson Plan

ARU Academy • Cohort 001

Trainer-Only Resource

Lesson Overview

Module 2 establishes the professional behaviour expected from every ARU student and qualified officer.

The lesson should make clear that technical ability does not excuse dishonesty, poor conduct, disrespect or an unwillingness to develop.

Lesson Module 2 — Professional Standards
Recommended Duration 75 minutes
Current Group Two ARU students
Delivery Style Discussion, activities and reflection
Assessment Observation and knowledge check
Pass Standard Satisfactory participation and 80% quiz score

Trainer Preparation

  • Confirm both students completed Module 1
  • Confirm both students have read Module 2
  • Prepare the Module 2 knowledge check
  • Prepare an attendance record
  • Review current Academy conduct expectations
  • Prepare examples of professional and poor conduct
  • Prepare a short reflection task
  • Confirm the trainer assessment form is available

Required Resources

  • Module 2 student page
  • Student Handbook
  • Module 2 knowledge check
  • Attendance record
  • Trainer assessment form
  • Discord voice channel or classroom location
  • Notes area for student reflection tasks

Learning Outcomes

By the end of the lesson, each student should be able to:

  • Explain professionalism within ARU.
  • Describe the importance of integrity.
  • Understand personal accountability.
  • Communicate respectfully during disagreement.
  • Explain why teamwork and trust are essential.
  • Receive and apply constructive feedback.
  • Understand attendance and commitment expectations.
  • Recognise confidentiality requirements.
  • Raise concerns through the correct channels.

Suggested Lesson Structure

Stage Time Trainer Action
Introduction 5 minutes Explain why professional standards are assessed throughout every Academy module.
Professionalism and Integrity 15 minutes Discuss behaviour, honesty, trust and responsibility.
Communication and Teamwork 15 minutes Discuss respectful communication, teamwork and disagreement.
Feedback and Accountability 10 minutes Explain how students should receive and apply feedback.
Scenario Discussions 20 minutes Deliver the scenarios and ask each student to explain their view.
Reflection and Review 10 minutes Complete the reflection task, review questions and explain the knowledge check.

Opening Script

Suggested wording

“Module 2 focuses on professional standards.

ARU officers are judged on more than tactical or technical ability. Your attitude, honesty, communication and ability to work with others will be assessed throughout the Academy.

A technically capable officer can still be unsuitable for ARU if they cannot act professionally, accept feedback or maintain trust.”

Stage 1 — Professionalism

Explain that professionalism applies during:

  • Academy lessons
  • Practical training
  • Operational roleplay
  • Discord discussions
  • Briefings and debriefings
  • Interaction with other departments
  • Use of internal systems and forms

A professional ARU student should:

  • Arrive prepared.
  • Listen when others are speaking.
  • Communicate respectfully.
  • Remain calm during disagreement.
  • Follow reasonable instructions.
  • Represent the unit positively.
  • Separate personal issues from professional duties.

Discussion — Technical Skill Versus Attitude

Ask both students:

Question Would you rather work with an officer who has excellent technical knowledge but a poor attitude, or an inexperienced officer who listens, learns and supports the team?

Expected learning points

  • Technical knowledge can be developed.
  • Poor attitude damages trust.
  • ARU depends upon teamwork.
  • Students must be willing to learn.
  • Professional conduct remains essential after qualification.

Stage 2 — Integrity

Explain that integrity means being honest even when admitting something may be uncomfortable.

Examples include:

  • Reporting mistakes promptly.
  • Giving accurate accounts.
  • Being honest about attendance.
  • Not exaggerating experience.
  • Completing assessments independently.
  • Not hiding another officer's error.
  • Using restricted systems appropriately.
Key message A training mistake can usually be corrected. Dishonesty damages the trust required for someone to remain within ARU.

Scenario One — Hidden Mistake

A student makes an error during training. The trainer does not notice, and the student believes they can avoid mentioning it.

Ask:

  • What should the student do?
  • Why should the mistake be reported?
  • Would the original error automatically mean failure?
  • What would make the matter more serious?

Expected learning points

  • The student should report the error.
  • The mistake can be used for development.
  • Concealing the error damages trust.
  • Dishonesty may become more serious than the original mistake.

Stage 3 — Accountability

Students are responsible for:

  • Attendance
  • Preparation
  • Completing required work
  • Raising problems early
  • Applying feedback
  • Explaining decisions
  • Maintaining equipment properly
  • Arranging catch-up learning
Trainer prompt What is the difference between explaining why something happened and making excuses?

Expected learning point

Accountability means recognising the circumstances while still taking responsibility and agreeing how to improve.

Stage 4 — Communication and Teamwork

Explain that professional communication includes:

  • Listening before responding.
  • Using clear and respectful language.
  • Asking for clarification when needed.
  • Avoiding unnecessary arguments.
  • Using the correct radio or Discord channel.
  • Giving useful information without dominating discussion.
  • Raising concerns professionally.

Effective team members:

  • Share relevant information.
  • Support colleagues.
  • Carry out assigned responsibilities.
  • Ask for assistance when required.
  • Resolve disagreements professionally.
  • Place team success above personal recognition.

Scenario Two — Disagreement During Training

Two students disagree during an exercise. One begins criticising the other in front of the group and refuses to continue working with them.

Ask:

  • What standards are not being met?
  • How should the disagreement be handled?
  • When should a trainer intervene?
  • How can the students rebuild trust?

Expected learning points

  • The disagreement should remain respectful.
  • The issue should be discussed calmly.
  • Personal criticism should be avoided.
  • The trainer should intervene if the exercise is disrupted.
  • Both students should agree a professional way forward.

Stage 5 — Receiving Feedback

Students should be taught to:

  1. Listen fully.
  2. Avoid becoming immediately defensive.
  3. Ask for clarification.
  4. Reflect on the issue.
  5. Agree an improvement action.
  6. Apply the feedback later.
  7. Request extra support where needed.

Explain that students may respectfully question feedback, but should focus on the issue rather than attacking the trainer.

Scenario Three — Rejecting Feedback

A trainer gives a student feedback following an exercise. The student immediately argues, says the trainer is wrong and refuses to discuss improvement.

Ask:

  • What standard is not being met?
  • How should the student respond instead?
  • Can a student disagree professionally?
  • What should happen if the behaviour continues?

Expected learning points

  • The student should listen first.
  • Clarification can be requested respectfully.
  • Repeated refusal to accept feedback may affect progression.
  • The focus should remain on development.

Stage 6 — Attendance and Commitment

Explain that students should:

  • Attend wherever reasonably possible.
  • Provide advance notice when unavailable.
  • Arrive prepared and on time.
  • Complete assigned work.
  • Participate meaningfully.
  • Arrange catch-up learning.
  • Raise difficulties early.

Genuine absence should be managed fairly. The concern is repeated unexplained absence or failure to engage.

Stage 7 — Confidentiality and Internal Conduct

Students must not share:

  • Assessment answers
  • Trainer resources
  • Scenario injects
  • Restricted documents
  • Personal student records
  • Passwords
  • Restricted screenshots
  • Internal command discussions

Professional standards also apply to:

  • Discord
  • Direct messages
  • Google Forms
  • Internal website pages
  • Written reports

Student Reflection Task

Ask each student to answer:

Reflection question What professional standard will you personally commit to during Cohort 001, and how will you demonstrate it?

Each response should include one practical commitment, such as:

  • Arriving prepared
  • Supporting the other student
  • Asking for feedback
  • Reporting mistakes honestly
  • Completing work on time

Keep the response for review during the final Academy interview.

Review Questions

  1. What does professionalism mean within ARU?
  2. Why is integrity essential?
  3. What does accountability mean?
  4. How should feedback be received?
  5. How should disagreement be raised?
  6. What should happen after a missed lesson?
  7. Why is teamwork important?
  8. What information must remain confidential?
  9. Does technical ability excuse poor conduct?
  10. How can poor behaviour affect the wider unit?

Expected Answers

  1. Consistently behaving appropriately during training, operations and internal communication.
  2. Integrity creates trust and ensures mistakes or concerns are reported honestly.
  3. Taking responsibility for actions, attendance, work and development.
  4. Listen, clarify, reflect and apply the feedback.
  5. Calmly, respectfully and through the correct channel.
  6. Notify the trainer and arrange catch-up learning.
  7. ARU relies upon trust, communication and shared performance.
  8. Restricted documents, answers, records, passwords and trainer materials.
  9. No. Technical ability does not excuse poor conduct.
  10. It can damage trust, morale, reputation and operational teamwork.

Instructor Observation Criteria

Professionalism Behaves appropriately throughout.
Integrity Demonstrates honesty and openness.
Communication Speaks respectfully and listens.
Teamwork Supports the other student.
Reflection Identifies meaningful development.
Feedback Responds appropriately to guidance.

Module Outcomes

Pass

Student participated appropriately, understood the professional standards and completed the required assessment.

Pass with Development Points

Student may progress but must address clearly identified development areas.

Remedial

Student requires further discussion, reflection or reassessment before progression.

Incomplete

Required attendance, reflection or assessment remains outstanding.

Unsuccessful

Student has not met the required standard after appropriate support and review.

Common Trainer Errors

  • Turning the lesson into a lecture.
  • Reading the student page word-for-word.
  • Ignoring poor behaviour during the lesson.
  • Allowing one student to dominate.
  • Confusing respectful challenge with disrespect.
  • Failing to record development points.
  • Using feedback as punishment rather than development.

Closing Script

Suggested wording

“That concludes Module 2.

Professional standards are not limited to this lesson. Your conduct, attitude, communication and response to feedback will continue to be assessed throughout the Academy.

Before Module 3, complete the knowledge check and reflect on the personal standard you have committed to maintaining.”
