Module 1 — Welcome to ARU

Code44 Armed Response Unit Academy

Cohort 001

Foundation Module

Module Overview

Module 1 introduces recruits to the Armed Response Unit, the ARU Academy and the standards expected throughout training.

By the end of this module, recruits should understand how the course is structured, who is responsible for their development and what they must demonstrate before being considered for operational qualification.

Estimated Duration 45–60 minutes
Delivery Method Classroom briefing and guided portal tour
Assessment Knowledge check and instructor confirmation
Pass Standard 80% knowledge-check score

Learning Objectives

By completing this module, recruits will be able to:

  • Describe the purpose and responsibilities of the Armed Response Unit.
  • Identify the ARU chain of command and training structure.
  • Explain the standards expected of an ARU recruit.
  • Navigate the Internal Database and ARU Academy.
  • Describe the route from recruit status to operational qualification.
  • Understand the importance of confidentiality, accountability and professional conduct.

1. Welcome to the Armed Response Unit

The Code44 Armed Response Unit provides specialist armed-policing support to the wider service. ARU personnel are expected to operate professionally, communicate clearly and make considered decisions during demanding incidents.

Membership of ARU is not simply permission to carry specialist equipment. It represents an increased level of responsibility, accountability and commitment to training.

2. ARU Academy Purpose

The ARU Academy exists to ensure that every recruit receives consistent training and is assessed against the same standard.

The Academy combines:

  • Classroom learning
  • Professional standards education
  • Legal and policy awareness
  • Communications training
  • Equipment familiarisation
  • Scenario-based roleplay assessments
  • Instructor feedback and mentoring
  • Final knowledge and practical assessments

Progression is not automatic. Recruits must complete the required learning, attend training and meet the relevant assessment standards.

4. Standards Expected of Recruits

  • Professionalism: behave appropriately and represent the unit positively.
  • Integrity: be honest about mistakes, limitations and training performance.
  • Accountability: take responsibility for decisions and actions.
  • Communication: provide clear information and listen to instructions.
  • Teamwork: support other recruits and contribute to collective performance.
  • Attendance: attend scheduled sessions or provide reasonable notice.
  • Development: accept feedback and demonstrate improvement.
  • Confidentiality: keep internal materials and unit information within authorised channels.

Recruits should raise training questions with their assigned instructor or mentor. Conduct, welfare or serious concerns should be escalated through the appropriate ARU command structure.

6. Using the Internal Database

Recruits must be able to locate and use:

  • The ARU Academy homepage
  • Current training modules
  • Student resources
  • Academy rules
  • Assessment criteria
  • Qualification standards
  • Relevant SOPs and guidance

Trainer-only materials, assessment answers and personnel-management records must not be accessed or shared without authorisation.

Confidentiality Notice

Internal training material is provided for Code44 ARU roleplay purposes. Recruits must not copy, publish or distribute restricted unit documents outside authorised channels.

Recruit Completion Checklist

  • Attend the Module 1 induction briefing
  • Review the Academy structure
  • Identify the training chain of command
  • Read the Academy standards
  • Complete the Internal Database walkthrough
  • Complete the Module 1 knowledge check
  • Receive instructor confirmation

Module Completion

Module 1 is complete once the recruit has attended the induction, reviewed the required information and achieved the required score in the knowledge check.
