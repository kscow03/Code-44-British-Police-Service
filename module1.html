Module Overview

Module 1 introduces you to the Armed Response Unit, the purpose of the ARU Academy and the standards expected throughout your training.

By the end of this module, you should understand how the Academy operates, what responsibilities come with joining ARU and what you must demonstrate before being considered for qualification.

1. Learning Objectives

After completing Module 1, you should be able to:

  • Explain the purpose of the Armed Response Unit.
  • Describe the purpose of the ARU Academy.
  • Understand the route from student to qualified operator.
  • Identify the standards expected throughout the course.
  • Know who to contact for training support.
  • Navigate the Student Area and Internal Database.
  • Understand that qualification is earned through assessment.

2. What Is the Armed Response Unit?

The Armed Response Unit provides specialist support to the wider Code44 Police Service during high-risk incidents and planned operations where trained armed resources are required.

ARU supports other departments and works within an established command structure. Its purpose is not simply to provide access to specialist vehicles or equipment.

ARU members are expected to demonstrate:

  • Professional conduct
  • Clear communication
  • Sound judgement
  • Teamwork
  • Reliability
  • Accountability
  • Commitment to continued development

3. Responsibility of an ARU Officer

Joining ARU brings additional responsibility. Students and qualified officers are expected to understand that specialist status does not remove the need for restraint, professionalism or accountability.

An ARU officer must be prepared to:

  • Explain their decisions.
  • Accept constructive feedback.
  • Report mistakes honestly.
  • Follow approved policy and command direction.
  • Support officers from other departments.
  • Maintain the standards required by the unit.
  • Continue training after qualification.

Technical ability is important, but it does not excuse poor conduct, dishonesty or an unwillingness to work as part of a team.

4. Purpose of the ARU Academy

The Academy exists to give every student a structured and consistent training experience.

Students should receive the same core learning, assessment opportunities and progression standards regardless of which approved trainer delivers the lesson.

The Academy combines:

  • Student reading and pre-learning
  • Trainer-led lessons
  • Classroom discussions
  • Knowledge checks
  • Practical exercises
  • Roleplay scenarios
  • Feedback and development
  • Final assessment

6. Recruit Expectations

Students are assessed on more than technical performance. Conduct and attitude are considered throughout the course.

Professionalism Behave appropriately during training, operations and internal communication.
Integrity Be honest about mistakes, experience, attendance and performance.
Accountability Take responsibility for your conduct, work and development.
Communication Listen carefully, ask questions and provide clear information.
Teamwork Support other students and contribute to shared success.
Development Accept feedback and demonstrate improvement.

Attendance

Students should:

  • Attend scheduled lessons wherever reasonably possible.
  • Notify trainers in advance when unavailable.
  • Complete required reading before training.
  • Arrive prepared and on time.
  • Arrange catch-up learning after missing a session.

8. Using the Internal Database

The Internal Database is the central location for approved ARU information, Academy learning and student resources.

Students should be able to locate:

  • The ARU Academy homepage
  • The Student Handbook
  • Current student modules
  • The course timetable
  • The SOP Library

9. Assessment and Progression

Attendance alone does not guarantee progression or qualification.

Students may be assessed through:

  • Knowledge checks
  • Classroom participation
  • Written exercises
  • Practical demonstrations
  • Scenario-based roleplay
  • Instructor observation
  • Final assessment

Possible outcomes include:

Pass The required standard has been met.
Pass with Development Points Progression is permitted with areas to improve.
Remedial Training Further learning is required before progression.
Incomplete Required attendance or work remains outstanding.
Unsuccessful The required standard has not been met.
