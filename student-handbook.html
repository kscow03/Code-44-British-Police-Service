1. Welcome to the ARU Academy

Welcome to the Code44 Police Service Armed Response Unit Academy.

Congratulations on being selected to begin training as an Authorised Firearms Officer.

Selection for the Academy does not mean that qualification is automatic. It provides you with an opportunity to demonstrate the professionalism, judgement, commitment and teamwork expected from an ARU officer.

Throughout the programme you will complete structured lessons, practical exercises, knowledge checks, scenario-based training and final assessments.

You are not expected to know everything when you begin. You are expected to listen, ask questions, accept feedback and demonstrate improvement.

2. Academy Mission

The purpose of the ARU Academy is to develop reliable and professional officers who can contribute effectively to the Armed Response Unit.

The Academy aims to produce officers who are:

  • Professional
  • Accountable
  • Reliable
  • Team-focused
  • Capable of communicating clearly
  • Willing to learn and develop
  • Able to explain their decisions
  • Committed to supporting the wider police service

3. Academy Values

Every lesson, assessment and progression decision is based upon the following values.

Professionalism Maintain an appropriate and credible standard throughout training, operations and internal communication.
Integrity Be honest about mistakes, attendance, knowledge and performance.
Accountability Accept responsibility for your conduct, work and decisions.
Teamwork Support fellow students and contribute to collective success.
Respect Treat trainers, colleagues and members of the wider service fairly.
Development Accept feedback, reflect and demonstrate improvement.

4. Course Structure

Training is delivered through a structured pathway. Students should complete modules in the order directed by the training team.

  • Student Handbook
  • Module 1 — Welcome to ARU
  • Module 2 — Professional Standards
  • Module 3 — Legal and Decision-Making
  • Module 4 — Equipment and Resources
  • Module 5 — Communications
  • Module 6 — Incident Management
  • Module 7 — Teamwork and Leadership
  • Module 8 — Final Assessment
  • Graduation and Qualification Decision

Modules may include classroom teaching, independent reading, discussion, practical exercises, roleplay scenarios and formal assessments.

5. Attendance and Commitment

Regular attendance is essential because each module builds upon previous learning.

Students are expected to:

  • Attend scheduled lessons wherever reasonably possible.
  • Notify a trainer in advance when unavailable.
  • Arrive on time and prepared.
  • Complete any required reading before the session.
  • Participate meaningfully during training.
  • Arrange catch-up learning after missing a lesson.
  • Complete assessments within agreed timescales.

Repeated unexplained absence or failure to complete required work may delay progression or result in removal from the Academy.

6. Student Conduct

Students represent the Armed Response Unit throughout the Academy.

Students must:

  • Behave professionally.
  • Follow reasonable trainer instructions.
  • Communicate respectfully.
  • Use appropriate channels for questions or concerns.
  • Support fellow students.
  • Respect rank and command responsibilities.
  • Avoid disrupting lessons or assessments.
  • Separate personal disagreements from professional duties.

Technical ability does not excuse dishonesty, disrespect, poor attitude or repeated unwillingness to improve.

7. Confidentiality and Internal Access

Academy resources are provided for authorised Code44 ARU use.

Students must not share:

  • Restricted SOPs or internal documents.
  • Assessment questions or answers.
  • Trainer-only lesson plans.
  • Scenario injects or marking guidance.
  • Personal student records.
  • Password or access information.
  • Screenshots from restricted pages.

Students must not attempt to bypass restrictions or access the Trainers Area.

8. Assessments

Assessment takes place throughout the course and is not limited to the final exercise.

Assessment methods may include:

  • Knowledge checks
  • Classroom discussion
  • Written decision exercises
  • Practical demonstrations
  • Scenario-based roleplay
  • Instructor observation
  • Professional interviews
  • Final assessment exercises

Students must complete assessments honestly and independently unless group work has been authorised.

9. Feedback and Development

Feedback is provided to help students improve and should not automatically be treated as personal criticism.

When receiving feedback, students should:

  1. Listen fully.
  2. Ask questions if anything is unclear.
  3. Reflect upon the issue identified.
  4. Agree an improvement action where appropriate.
  5. Apply the feedback during future training.
  6. Ask for additional support if required.

Students are also encouraged to provide respectful feedback about the Academy and its delivery.

10. Progression and Outcomes

Qualification is based upon overall performance throughout the Academy.

Following a module or assessment, an instructor may record:

Pass The required standard has been met.
Pass with Development Points The student may progress but must address identified areas.
Remedial Training Additional learning is required before progression.
Incomplete Required attendance, work or assessment remains outstanding.
Unsuccessful The required standard has not been met after appropriate consideration.

A student requiring remedial training should be given clear feedback, reasonable support and an agreed reassessment pathway.

11. Student Support

Students should raise concerns early rather than allowing difficulties to continue.

Support may be requested from:

  • An allocated trainer
  • An assigned mentor
  • The lead trainer
  • An Operational Firearms Commander
  • The ARU command team

Students should speak to a trainer if they:

  • Do not understand part of the course.
  • Cannot attend a scheduled session.
  • Require additional practice.
  • Have concerns about their progress.
  • Experience conflict within the cohort.
  • Need welfare support.

12. Student Lesson Checklist

Before every lesson:

  • Read the required student module
  • Complete any assigned pre-learning
  • Check the course timetable
  • Join the correct Discord or training channel
  • Arrive on time
  • Bring any required equipment
  • Raise questions if unsure
  • Maintain professional conduct

Cohort 001 Commitment

By accepting a place within Cohort 001, every student commits to:

  • Giving their best effort.
  • Acting professionally.
  • Supporting fellow students.
  • Accepting constructive feedback.
  • Demonstrating integrity.
  • Completing required learning.
  • Upholding the reputation of the Armed Response Unit.

Final Message

“The Academy does not exist to catch you out. It exists to prepare you for the responsibility of serving within the Armed Response Unit. Every lesson, assessment and piece of feedback is designed to help you become a more professional, reliable and capable officer. Commit yourself fully, support your cohort and take pride in your development.”
