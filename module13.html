ARU Academy — Final Stage

Final Assessment

Module Overview

Module 13 is the final stage of the ARU Academy.

Students progressing to this stage have completed the required development modules and have been recommended as ready for final assessment.

The purpose of Module 13 is to determine whether the student can independently and consistently demonstrate the standards expected of a qualified Code44 Armed Response Unit officer.

Final Assessment Principle

The final assessment is not designed to catch students out.

It is designed to confirm that the knowledge, professional standards, communication, decision-making and practical abilities developed throughout the Academy can be applied consistently without excessive trainer assistance.

Assessment Areas

Professionalism Professional behaviour, attitude and conduct throughout the assessment.
Integrity Honest communication, accountability and willingness to acknowledge mistakes.
Knowledge Understanding of the core learning developed throughout the Academy.
Firearms Safety Safe and controlled handling within Code44 training and operational scenarios.
Communication Clear, relevant and timely communication.
Teamwork Effective contribution to ARV crew and wider team performance.
Decision-Making Reasonable and explainable decisions based on the information available.
Reassessment Recognition and response to changing circumstances.
Operational Awareness Awareness of subjects, colleagues, civilians, resources and the wider incident.
Accountability Ability to explain decisions and actions during post-assessment debrief.

Assessment Format

The exact assessment format is determined by the ARU training team.

Students may be assessed through a combination of:

  • Knowledge questioning.
  • A written or online knowledge assessment.
  • Practical ARU scenarios.
  • ARV deployment scenarios.
  • Vehicle-related incidents.
  • Containment or search scenarios.
  • Building-operation scenarios.
  • Integrated multi-unit incidents.
  • Post-incident handovers.
  • Trainer-led debrief questioning.

Not every student will necessarily receive the exact same scenario.

Assessment Scenarios

Final assessment scenarios are restricted.

Students will not be provided with scenario details, trainer injects, expected responses or marking guidance in advance.

This ensures that the assessment measures understanding and decision-making rather than the ability to memorise a particular scenario.

What Students Should Revise

Before attending the final assessment, students should revisit any Academy area they are uncertain about.

Revision should include:

  • ARU professional standards.
  • Integrity and accountability.
  • Decision-making and continuous reassessment.
  • Reported versus confirmed information.
  • ARU equipment, PPE and vehicle responsibilities.
  • Firearms safety and accountability.
  • ARV crew responsibilities.
  • Armed incident deployment principles.
  • Vehicle-incident principles.
  • Containment and search principles.
  • High-risk warrant awareness.
  • Building-operation and CQB principles.
  • Multi-unit and specialist-resource integration.
  • Casualty awareness.
  • Operational handovers and debrief accountability.

Independent Performance

Earlier Academy modules are developmental and trainers may provide coaching throughout practical exercises.

During final assessment, students should expect less trainer assistance.

Students are expected to:

  • Ask appropriate questions where information is unclear.
  • Recognise important information independently.
  • Communicate without constant prompting.
  • Work effectively with their crew.
  • Recognise when circumstances change.
  • Adapt decisions appropriately.
  • Acknowledge uncertainty where it exists.
  • Explain their actions afterwards.

Mistakes During Assessment

A single mistake does not automatically mean assessment failure.

Trainers will consider the seriousness of the issue, the circumstances, whether the student recognised it and how they responded afterwards.

Students should not attempt to hide mistakes or continue with an unsuitable decision simply because the assessment is underway.

Honest reassessment and correction may itself demonstrate good judgement.

Assessment Conduct

Students must maintain the same professional standards expected throughout the Academy.

This includes:

  • Following trainer instructions.
  • Maintaining professional conduct.
  • Respecting other students and roleplayers.
  • Operating within Code44 server rules.
  • Maintaining firearms and equipment discipline.
  • Being honest during debrief.
  • Not attempting to obtain restricted assessment material.

Restricted Assessment Material

The following information is restricted to authorised ARU trainers:

  • Final scenario details.
  • Scenario objectives and injects.
  • Expected answers.
  • Assessment scoring guidance.
  • Trainer marking sheets.
  • Critical assessment criteria.
  • Qualification recommendation guidance.

Students must not attempt to access or obtain restricted final-assessment material.

Possible Assessment Outcomes

PASS — Qualified

The student has demonstrated the required standard and is recommended for qualification as a Code44 ARU officer.

PASS WITH DEVELOPMENT POINTS

The required overall standard has been met, but limited development points remain and should continue to be monitored after qualification.

REASSESSMENT REQUIRED

One or more areas require further development before the qualification decision can be confirmed.

FURTHER TRAINING REQUIRED

The student has not yet demonstrated sufficient consistency and will complete additional targeted development before another assessment attempt.

After a Successful Assessment

Successful completion of Module 13 confirms completion of the ARU Academy.

Qualification does not mean development ends.

Qualified officers are expected to:

  • Continue maintaining ARU professional standards.
  • Attend refresher and development training.
  • Maintain knowledge of Code44 procedures.
  • Accept and apply operational feedback.
  • Maintain firearms and equipment discipline.
  • Support other ARU officers.
  • Continue developing specialist skills where authorised.

Qualification Does Not Grant Every Specialist Capability

Completion of the core ARU Academy qualifies the officer within the standard Code44 ARU role.

Additional specialist capabilities may require separate training, authorisation or approval.

These may include specialist precision capability, breaching roles, Counter Terrorism, VIP protection, rope access, fast-rope or other specialist functions.

Final Student Reminder

You have already completed the development required to reach this stage.

Final assessment is your opportunity to demonstrate that you can apply those standards consistently.

Do not attempt to guess what scenario is coming.

Focus on the information you actually have, communicate with your team, continually reassess the situation and be prepared to explain your decisions.

Module 13 Completion

Module 13 is complete once:

  • The required final knowledge element has been completed.
  • Required practical assessment scenarios have been completed.
  • The student's individual performance has been reviewed.
  • The final debrief has been completed.
  • The qualification decision has been recorded.
