How Progress Works

Each module is tracked individually. Tick a module to mark it as completed and automatically update the student's overall progress.

The progress bar is calculated from the number of completed modules out of 13.

Once all 13 modules have been completed, the student can be marked Qualified.

Shared Academy Database

All recruit information is stored in the central Academy database.

When one trainer updates a student's progress, the change is saved to Supabase and can appear on other trainer computers.
