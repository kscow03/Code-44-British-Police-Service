Academy Overview

The ARU Academy provides the structured training pathway for officers selected to begin development within the Armed Response Unit.

The Academy develops students from initial induction through professional standards, decision-making, equipment, firearms development and ARV operations before progressing into advanced operational scenarios and final qualification.

Students have access to approved learning modules and reference material. Practical training, coaching and assessment are delivered by authorised ARU trainers.

Not every module requires a formal classroom session. Students are expected to complete assigned pre-learning independently and arrive at practical training prepared to apply what they have learned.

Academy Structure

The Academy progressively develops officers through 13 modules.

Earlier modules establish the professional, legal and operational foundations required within ARU. Later modules increasingly focus on practical application, integrated incidents and independent performance.

Modules 12 and 13 represent the final development and assessment stages before qualification.

Student Notice

Students are expected to complete required pre-learning before practical training and should raise questions with an approved trainer whenever clarification is required.

Training sessions are delivered when students, trainers and appropriate resources are available within the city rather than through a fixed timetable.

Students should continue using completed Academy pages for revision throughout the course.

Qualification is not automatic. Students must meet the required standards across professionalism, knowledge, practical performance, communication, teamwork, decision-making and final assessment.

Module 13 Access

Students may read the Module 13 student information page for final assessment preparation.

Progression into the actual final assessment must be authorised by the ARU Training Team following satisfactory completion of Module 12.

Final assessment scenarios, marking criteria, expected responses and trainer guidance remain restricted.

Restricted Access

Students must not attempt to access trainer-only materials, assessment answers, marking guidance, scenario injects, assessment-bank information or personal recruit records.

Trainer resources must not be copied, distributed or shared outside authorised ARU training channels.
