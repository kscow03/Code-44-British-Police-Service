Academy Overview

The ARU Academy provides a structured training pathway for officers selected to begin development within the Armed Response Unit.

Students receive access to approved learning materials, course modules, revision resources and assessments. Trainers use a separate restricted area containing lesson plans, assessment guidance and recruit-management documents.

Student Notice

Students must complete required pre-learning before attending practical lessons and should raise questions with an approved trainer whenever clarification is required.

Qualification is not automatic. Students must meet the required standards across attendance, professionalism, knowledge, practical performance and final assessment.

Restricted Access

Students must not attempt to access trainer-only materials, assessment answers, marking guidance, hidden scenario information or personal recruit records.

Trainer resources must not be copied, distributed or shared outside authorised ARU training channels.
