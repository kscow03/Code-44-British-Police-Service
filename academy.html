Academy Overview

The ARU Academy provides a structured development pathway for officers selected to undertake Armed Response Unit training.

Students receive access to approved learning materials, Academy guidance, assessments and course resources. Trainers use a separate restricted area to manage lesson delivery, assessment and student progression.
